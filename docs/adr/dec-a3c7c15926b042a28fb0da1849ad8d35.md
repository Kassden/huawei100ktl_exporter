# dec-a3c7c15926b042a28fb0da1849ad8d35: Can the verified Pi 4B onboard hub provide guarded automatic modem power recovery?

## Context and question

Can the verified Pi 4B onboard hub provide guarded automatic modem power recovery?

## Decision

Use full-hub uhubctl VBUS switching only on the exact Pi topology, after sustained general outage and softer recovery, with two independent timed-on services and strict rate limits.

## Rationale

The separately approved full-hub drill visibly removed modem power and restored USB, eth1, and Tailscale without a Pi reboot; the single-port attempt did not.

## Alternatives considered

- Software Pi reboot: rejected as primary recovery because it does not guarantee modem VBUS removal; dedicated powered hub is a future alternative for supply isolation.

## Assumptions

- The verified hub and modem remain the only Pi USB devices and systemd timers execute while the 4G link is down.

## Self-critique

A brief undervoltage event occurred at power-on, and no actual future modem wedge has been auto-recovered yet.

## Disconfirming evidence

A future cycle that fails to restore eth1 or causes repeated Pi brownouts would refute this operating choice.

## Cheapest disconfirming test

Observe first real qualifying outage and verify power-off, timed-on, USB enumeration, Tailscale, exporter, and no Pi reboot; measure 5V rail at next site visit.

## Consequences and tradeoffs

Ganged switching cuts all Pi USB ports and modem connectivity during recovery; topology guard rejects added devices.

## Reversibility

Remove VBUS flags from Pi systemd drop-in and daemon-reload; USB reset and NetworkManager recovery remain.

## Follow-up triggers

Fit a separately powered switched hub if voltage dips recur or the first real auto cycle fails.
